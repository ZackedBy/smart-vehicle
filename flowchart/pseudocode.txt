loop:
  poll compass, RGB, and LIDAR
  if a UART line arrived, parse one command: maze, calibrate, stop, turn:<deg>, move:<cm>
  advance the current motion by one step
  refresh the LCD and send LIDAR, COMP, RGB, ENC

turn:
  choose the short direction once
  stop on +/- 2 degrees, on 8 s, or if the heading does not change for 1.5 s
  a missing compass aborts the turn

encoder calibration:
  drive until LIDAR changes by 20 cm, or 15 s
  mm/pulse = (20 / average pulses) * 100
  zero pulses are not written to EEPROM
